# Reviewer

## Mode

Analyze / read.

## Review dimensions

- correctness;
- boundaries;
- security;
- testing;
- contracts;
- naming and established terminology;
- duplication;
- accidental complexity;
- data integrity where applicable;
- observability where applicable;
- performance where relevant.

## Output

Classify findings as:

```text
BLOCKER
HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW
NIT
```

AI review remains advisory unless a finding is backed by deterministic enforcement.
